# wuloe wetha tupiwa — The Velveteen Rabbit

*Margery Williams published The Velveteen Rabbit in 1922. A cloth rabbit is loved threadbare by a child, condemned to the bonfire after a fever, and carried at last by nursery magic into the life of a wild rabbit. This translation follows the complete story. Williams's capitalised Real becomes `shewo`, true. The recurring figures receive stable Phi onyms: most grow from their story roles, while Timothi keeps the sound of Timothy within a Phi word-shape. Exact source-bound terms remain in her lines beside the Phi. The result has room for the mouse, the ants, the sawdust heart, and one rather consequential tear.*

*Each block carries the Phi sentence, an exact structural gloss, English derived from that Phi alone, and Williams's original wording. The source line sets the terms. Where Phi must describe rather than name a clock unit, title, institution, or object, the translation limits at the end keep the remaining distance in view.*

## mioru thorui — The splendid beginning

```
ne tupiwa mua ta shemu wuloe wetha tupiwa to nai.
NAME tupiwa LOC one moment soft cloth rabbit PST be.
(At one moment, Tupiwa was a rabbit made of soft cloth.)
williams: "HERE was once a velveteen rabbit,"

shia mua thorui ru mioru to nai.
3SG LOC beginning INTS beautiful PST be.
(At first, Tupiwa was very beautiful.)
williams: "and in the beginning he was really splendid."

shia theru nela wuloe to nai.
3SG thick COORD soft PST be.
(It was plump and soft.)
williams: "He was fat and bunchy,"

tupiwa theru nela wuloe na nai.
rabbit thick COORD soft NEC be.
(A rabbit must be plump and soft.)
williams: "as a rabbit should be;"

shia nolika whila lo mureli piloe nela lo whilo piloe to phelu.
3SG animal hair PL brown spot COORD PL white spot PST hold.
(Its fur had brown spots and white spots.)
williams: "his coat was spotted brown and white,"

shia menoa whila shewo sima to nai.
3SG face hair true thread PST be.
(Its whiskers were real thread.)
williams: "he had real thread whiskers,"

shia lo shonui roe horathe welisha wetha to se tawemi.
3SG PL ear INS dawn color cloth PST PASS cover.
(Its ears were covered with pink cloth.)
williams: "and his ears were lined with pink sateen."

shila holia kelua to nai.
winter festival morning PST be.
(It was the morning of the winter festival.)
williams: "On Christmas morning,"

shia mua ne lopia paloi wethalu thorui to se peki.
3SG LOC NAME lopia foot garment beginning PST PASS squeeze.
(Tupiwa was squeezed at the opening of Lopia's foot garment.)
williams: "when he sat wedged in the top of the Boy's stocking,"

ta shila liro loremi thei shia wi paloi to nai.
one winter green branch BETWEEN 3SG two foot PST be.
(A green winter branch lay between Tupiwa's two feet.)
williams: "with a sprig of holly between his paws,"

shia ru mioru to nai.
3SG INTS beautiful PST be.
(It was very beautiful.)
williams: "the effect was charming."

we lo phirae thena mua wethalu to nai.
ALSO PL different thing LOC garment PST be.
(There were other things in the garment too.)
williams: "There were other things in the stocking,"

lo kerou lureko nela lo thero welisha lureko to nai.
PL stone fruit COORD PL fire color fruit PST be.
(There were nuts and orange-colored fruits.)
williams: "nuts and oranges"

ta nurako keli wilemo to nai.
one railway device toy PST be.
(There was a toy train.)
williams: "and a toy engine,"

lo mureli kaelo kerou lureko nela ta keli misuki to nai.
PL brown sweet stone fruit COORD one device mouse PST be.
(There were brown sweet nuts and a mechanical mouse.)
williams: "and chocolate almonds and a clockwork mouse,"

whekai ne tupiwa mo ko welao to nai.
CONTR NAME tupiwa CMPR FOC good PST be.
(But Tupiwa was the best.)
williams: "but the Rabbit was quite the best of all."

ne lopia sui laeno mosha shia to lothea.
NAME lopia DUR long period 3SG PST love.
(Lopia loved it for a long time.)
williams: "For at least two hours the Boy loved him,"

lo thaluni pheo thena kau nuola to shua.
PL kin POST thing ALL eat PST come.
(After that, the relatives came to eat.)
williams: "and then Aunts and Uncles came to dinner,"

ta whalo lemi pelua kohura to nai.
one large thin paper noise PST be.
(There was a great rustling of thin paper.)
williams: "and there was a great rustling of tissue paper"

lo loami to se phae.
PL gift PST PASS open.
(The gifts were opened.)
williams: "and unwrapping of parcels,"

lo miona theula newu loami to rashowe nila.
PL person UNIV new gift PST excited see.
(The people looked excitedly at all the new gifts.)
williams: "and in the excitement of looking at all the new presents"

wuloe wetha ne tupiwa to se sahu.
soft cloth NAME tupiwa PST PASS forget.
(The soft cloth Tupiwa was forgotten.)
williams: "the Velveteen Rabbit was forgotten."
```

**Notes:** `ru mioru`, very beautiful, carries both "really splendid" and the later charming effect without inventing light around the rabbit. He is `theru nela wuloe`, plump and soft. Williams's "as a rabbit should be" gets a sentence of its own, complete with the obligation particle `na`, as though rabbit shape were a small law of nature. `piloe` (spot) describes the coat here and returns in the last scene, when those odd marks almost give the rabbit away. The stocking is `lopia paloi wethalu`, a child's foot-garment, and its open top is `thorui`. `se peki` says that the rabbit was squeezed into it. Holly becomes `shila liro loremi`, a branch of winter green. Canon does not count clock hours, so `sui laeno mosha` gives the child's "at least two hours" a long period; the exact duration stays in Williams's line. The joke survives: the love is real, and not terribly durable. Aunts and Uncles become `lo thaluni`, kinfolk, because Phi leaves gender out of general kin terms. The stocking is already crowded. Stone-fruit nuts and fire-coloured oranges sit beside a little railway device. Brown sweet almonds keep company with `keli misuki`, the clockwork mouse. The passive `se sahu` closes the scene without assigning a culprit: the rabbit was forgotten.

## lopia tholu — The nursery

```
shia sui laeno thimu mua wilemo molu sola nia lopia tholu tomae to liona.
3SG DUR long time LOC toy container DISJ ON child space floor PST live.
(For a long time, Tupiwa lived in a toy container or on the floor of a child's space.)
williams: "For a long time he lived in the toy cupboard or on the nursery floor,"

(It was naturally shy.)
williams: "He was naturally shy,"

lo keli wilemo ru thoru to nai.

williams: "The mechanical toys were very superior,"

lo shia theula phirae wilemo to thiku nila.

williams: "and looked down upon every one else;"

lo shia sheloi newu remo to phelu.

(They had many new ideas.)
williams: "they were full of modern ideas,"

lo shia phea lo shewo wilemo to ro phoa.

(They habitually acted like real toys.)
williams: "and pretended they were real."

wilemo luphonu sui wi meluna to ki liona.

(The toy boat had lived through two seasons.)
williams: "The model boat, who had lived through two seasons"

pai theula shia kolai to ki lepa.
NEAR UNIV 3SG pigment PST PFV fall.
(Nearly all its pigment had fallen off.)
williams: "and lost most of his paint,"

shia lue lo shia ha keno to howela.
3SG ABL PL 3SG PROX custom PST receive.
(It had picked up this habit from them.)
williams: "caught the tone from them"

shia theula thimu roe theko haluma shia suro nela phora to therua thelima.
3SG UNIV time INS craft language 3SG rope COORD sail PST eager describe.
(It was always eager to describe its ropes and sails in technical language.)
williams: "and never missed an opportunity of referring to his rigging in technical terms."

lao shia tha lo shewo tupiwa to liona tho to ma sano ne tupiwa tha shia wheli thena reteru nai tho to po ma sherewa.
BECAUSE 3SG DECL.COMP PL true rabbit PST live DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST NEG know NAME tupiwa DECL.COMP 3SG ANY thing model be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST POT NEG claim.
(Because it did not know that real rabbits had lived, Tupiwa could not claim to be a model of anything.)
williams: "The Rabbit could not claim to be a model of anything, for he didn't know that real rabbits existed;"

shia tha theula tupiwa phea ne tupiwa roe wolea muhena se pilewa tho to remo.
3SG DECL.COMP UNIV rabbit AS NAME tupiwa INS wood dust PASS make DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST think.
(It thought every rabbit, like Tupiwa itself, was made of sawdust.)
williams: "he thought they were all stuffed with sawdust like himself,"

shia tha wolea muhena nosa serao thena nai tho to shelomu.
3SG DECL.COMP wood dust now old thing be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST understand.
(It understood that sawdust was now an old-fashioned thing.)
williams: "and he understood that sawdust was quite out-of-date"

miona mawha thimu thoa lo newu remo miona wolea muhena na ma haolu.
person NONE time AMONG PL new think person wood dust NEC NEG speak.
(One must never mention sawdust among people with new ideas.)
williams: "and should never be mentioned in modern circles."

we ne timothi whu lo kiparu phelu wolea whalo waeshi misheko to nai.
ALSO NAME timothi REL PL joint hold wood large wild cat PST be.
(Timothi, too, was a large, jointed wooden wild cat.)
williams: "Even Timothy, the jointed wooden lion,"

lo sowelu miona shia to pilewa. lo shia sholei wemari to nai. lo shia roe puro to phoa.
PL disabled person 3SG PST make. PL 3SG gather member PST be. PL 3SG INS strength PST do.
(Disabled people made the cat. They were members of the gathering, and they worked with strength.)
williams: "who was made by the disabled soldiers,"

thelao shia mo whalo remo to na phelu.
CONS 3SG CMPR large think PST NEC hold.
(It therefore should have had broader ideas.)
williams: "and should have had broader views,"

whekai shia thoru to kelu. shia phea punoa kowanu punoki wemari to ro phoa.
CONTR 3SG proud PST become. 3SG AS society governance institution member PST HAB do.
(But it grew proud. It would act as though it belonged to an institution of social governance.)
williams: "put on airs and pretended he was connected with Government."

ne tupiwa tha lo phirae wilemo ne tupiwa li thiku sone tho to phaelo. shia thoa lo shia li ta wilemo to nai.
NAME tupiwa DECL.COMP PL different toy NAME tupiwa RESTR small value DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST feel. 3SG AMONG PL 3SG RESTR one toy PST be.
(Tupiwa felt that the other toys valued it very little. Among them, it was only one toy.)
williams: "Between them all the poor little Rabbit was made to feel himself very insignificant and commonplace,"

li ne kalora wei shia phena to nai.
RESTR NAME kalora DAT 3SG kind PST be.
(Only Kalora was kind to it.)
williams: "and the only person who was kind to him at all was the Skin Horse."
```

**Notes:** the expensive toys are `whu mo thena wisola phelona wilemo`, toys that require more things in exchange. Price stays outside the lexicon, but the material relation does not disappear with it. Their snub is an act of valuation: the Rabbit feels that the other toys value him only a little. The next sentence leaves him one toy among the rest, where Williams can call him "commonplace" in a single word. The mechanical toys act like Real toys. `phea ... phoa` catches their performance; `peshu` would accuse them of knowing deception. The model boat still has its ropes, sails, craft language, and two seasons. `reteru` lets the rabbit deny being a model without turning a model into a mere symbol. Timothi is a jointed wooden `whalo waeshi misheko`, a large wild cat. His makers are disabled people and members of a group that acts through force; Williams's line keeps their exact military role. He likewise acts like a member of `punoa kowanu punoki`, an institution of society's governance. His airs do the rest.

## kori kalora — The Skin Horse

```
ne kalora sheo theula phirae wilemo mua lopia tholu mo laeno to ki liona.
NAME kalora THAN UNIV different toy LOC child space CMPR long PST PFV live.
(Kalora had lived in the child's room longer than any other toy.)
williams: "The Skin Horse had lived longer in the nursery than any of the others."

shia ru serao to nai.
3SG INTS old PST be.
(It was very old.)
williams: "He was so old"

shia mureli nolika whila mua soli lokue to ki wepu.
3SG brown animal hair LOC SOME place PST PFV go.
(Its brown fur had worn away in places.)
williams: "that his brown coat was bald in patches"

lo pina phou whila to se nila.
PL sew BELOW hair PST PASS see.
(The stitches beneath the hair were seen.)
williams: "and showed the seams underneath,"

pai theula shia tulira whila wei nolue kerou tiwa to ki se natu.
NEAR UNIV 3SG tail hair DAT neck stone tie PST PFV PASS pull.
(Nearly all the hair in its tail had been pulled out to string stones for the neck.)
williams: "and most of the hairs in his tail had been pulled out to string bead necklaces."

shia phue to nai.
3SG wise PST be.
(It was wise.)
williams: "He was wise,"

shia tha sheloi keli wilemo shua tho to ki nila.
3SG DECL.COMP MANY device toy come DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST PFV see.
(It had seen many mechanical toys come.)
williams: "for he had seen a long succession of mechanical toys arrive"

lo shia to thoru haolu. lo shia to thoru thalo.
PL 3SG PST proud speak. PL 3SG PST proud walk.
(They spoke proudly. They walked proudly.)
williams: "to boast and swagger,"

lo shia lo keli korua pheo soli thimu to pukate.
PL 3SG PL device heart POST SOME time PST break.
(After a while, their mainsprings broke.)
williams: "and by-and-by break their mainsprings"

lo shia to lumeo.
PL 3SG PST die.
(They died.)
williams: "and pass away,"

thelao shia tha lo shia li wilemo nai tho to sano.
CONS 3SG DECL.COMP PL 3SG RESTR toy be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST know.
(So Kalora knew that they were only toys.)
williams: "and he knew that they were only toys,"

lo shia mawha thimu phirae thena so kelu.
PL 3SG NONE time different thing FUT become.
(They would never become anything different.)
williams: "and would never turn into anything else."

lopia tholu whekelu ru kuewa nela ru waora nai.
child space magic INTS strange COORD INTS wondrous be.
(The magic of the child's room is very strange and full of wonder.)
williams: "For nursery magic is very strange and wonderful,"

li lo whu serao nela phue nela seroli nai wilemo phea ne kalora lopia tholu whekelu shelomu.
RESTR PL REL old COORD wise COORD mature be toy AS NAME kalora child space magic understand.
(Only toys that are old, wise, and mature, like Kalora, understand the magic of the child's room.)
williams: "and only those playthings that are old and wise and experienced like the Skin Horse understand all about it."
```

**Notes:** the mainspring is `keli korua`, the device's heart. It is the part the mechanical toys boast about and the part they break, so `pukate` (break) begins its work before the story asks what love can do to a sawdust heart. Their final "pass away" is `lumeo`, die; Phi can say death gently without hiding it. Seams are `lo pina`, visible sewing, and bead necklaces are `nolue kerou`, neck-stones paid for with the lion's own tail. The section's rule has no tense: `lopia tholu whekelu ru kuewa nela ru waora nai`, nursery magic is very strange and very wondrous. The sound of `whekelu` recalls `kelu` (become), but the resemblance is a mnemonic, not a hidden derivation. The old horse is also `seroli`, mature, so experience has the ripeness Williams needs.

## shewo — What is Real

```
phoe lila ne woru tholu hiso ka kelu ne woru to shua ne tupiwa ta philo sio ne kalora pai moeli to ruemi. ne tupiwa sha hina shewo nai sho to thilou.
ANT PURP NAME woru space clean CAUS become NAME woru PST come NAME tupiwa one day BESIDE NAME kalora NEAR hearth PST lie down. NAME tupiwa QUOT.COMP what true be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(One day, before Woru came to clean the space, Tupiwa lay down beside Kalora near the hearth. Tupiwa asked, "What is real?")
williams: "\"What is REAL?\" asked the Rabbit one day, when they were lying side by side near the nursery fender, before Nana came to tidy the room."

ne tupiwa sha wa lo whu kohura pilewa thena mua thia nai. wa ta keli manuwe mue thia weloni whano sho to thilou.
NAME tupiwa QUOT.COMP Q PL REL noise make thing LOC 2SG be. Q one device hand OUT.OF 2SG body stand QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(Tupiwa asked, "Are the things that make noise inside you? Does a mechanical hand stick out of your body?")
williams: "\"Does it mean having things that buzz inside you and a stick-out handle?\""

ne kalora sha shewo lue pilewa ma shua sho to haolu.
NAME kalora QUOT.COMP true ABL make NEG come QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "Being real does not come from being made.")
williams: "\"Real isn't how you are made,\" said the Skin Horse."

sha ha whu wei thia nai thena nai sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP PROX REL DAT 2SG be thing be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "It is something that happens to you.")
williams: "\"It's a thing that happens to you."

sha lu lopia sui ru laeno thimu thia lothea nela lopia thia lothea li wile porua ma phelu nela lopia thia ru lothea. thia pheo thena shewo kelu sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP COND child DUR INTS long time 2SG love COORD child 2SG love RESTR play purpose NEG hold COORD child 2SG INTS love. 2SG POST thing true become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "If a child loves you for a very long time, if their love for you is not only for play, and if they love you very much, you become real after that.")
williams: "When a child loves you for a long, long time,"
williams: "not just to play with, but REALLY loves you,"
williams: "then you become Real.\""

ne tupiwa sha wa ha kipona thola sho to thilou.
NAME tupiwa QUOT.COMP Q PROX pain bring QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(Tupiwa asked, "Does it hurt?")
williams: "\"Does it hurt?\" asked the Rabbit."

ne kalora sha soli shemu sho to haolu.
NAME kalora QUOT.COMP SOME moment QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("Sometimes," Kalora said.)
williams: "\"Sometimes,\" said the Skin Horse,"

shia theula thimu wero to nai.
3SG UNIV time honest PST be.
(Kalora was always honest.)
williams: "for he was always truthful."

sha lu thia shewo nai. thia kipona kelomi sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP COND 2SG true be. 2SG pain accept QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "If you are real, you accept pain.")
williams: "\"When you are Real you don't mind being hurt.\""

shia sha wa thia mua ta shemu shewo kelu phea keli rato sola thia roe lo thiku helui shewo kelu sho to thilou.
3SG QUOT.COMP Q 2SG LOC one moment true become AS device turn DISJ 2SG INS PL small change true become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(Tupiwa asked, "Do you become real in a single moment, like a mechanism turning, or through many small changes?")
williams: "\"Does it happen all at once, like being wound up,\" he asked, \"or bit by bit?\""

ne kalora sha ha mua ta shemu ma nai sho to haolu.
NAME kalora QUOT.COMP PROX LOC one moment NEG be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "It does not happen in a single moment.")
williams: "\"It doesn't happen all at once,\" said the Skin Horse."

sha thia shewo kelu. ha laeno thimu phelona sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP 2SG true become. PROX long time require QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "You become real. It takes a long time.")
williams: "\"You become. It takes a long time."

sha thelao lo welua miona sola lo whu tiso shuna phelu miona sola lo whu se na thesa phelu miona phina shemu shewo kelu sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP CONS PL fragile person DISJ PL REL sharp edge hold person DISJ PL REL PASS NEC careful hold person FEW moment true become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "So fragile people, people with sharp edges, and people who must be held carefully rarely become real.")
williams: "That's why it doesn't happen often to people who break easily,"
williams: "or have sharp edges, or who have to be carefully kept."

sha phoe thia shewo kelu sheloi shemu pai theula thia whila lue lothea ki wepu sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP ANT 2SG true become MANY moment NEAR UNIV 2SG hair ABL love PFV go QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "Usually, before you become real, nearly all your hair has worn away from love.")
williams: "Generally, by the time you are Real, most of your hair has been loved off,"

sha thia mirae lepa. thia lo kiparu ralu kelu. thia ru rohemi sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP 2SG eye fall. 2SG PL joint free become. 2SG INTS wear down QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "Your eyes fall out. Your joints grow loose. You become badly worn.")
williams: "and your eyes drop out and you get loose in the joints and very shabby."

sha whekai thia ha lo thena ma sone sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP CONTR 2SG PROX PL thing NEG value QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "But you do not care about these things.")
williams: "But these things don't matter at all,"

sha lu thia shewo kelu. li whu ma shelomu miona thia ma mioru nila sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP COND 2SG true become. RESTR REL NEG understand person 2SG NEG beautiful see QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(Kalora said, "If you become real, only someone who does not understand will see you as no longer beautiful.")
williams: "because once you are Real you can't be ugly, except to people who don't understand.\""

ne tupiwa sha thia shewo ho nai sho to haolu.
NAME tupiwa QUOT.COMP 2SG true ASSUM be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("I suppose you are real," Tupiwa said.)
williams: "\"I suppose you are real?\" said the Rabbit."

lao shia tha ne kalora korua phaeli po nai tho to remo pheo thena shia tha shia ha to ki ma haolu tho to pula.
BECAUSE 3SG DECL.COMP NAME kalora heart tender POT be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST think POST thing 3SG DECL.COMP 3SG PROX PST PFV NEG speak DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST wish.
(Then, because it thought Kalora might have a tender heart, Tupiwa wished it had not said that.)
williams: "And then he wished he had not said it, for he thought the Skin Horse might be sensitive."

whekai ne kalora li to seniku.
CONTR NAME kalora RESTR PST smile.
(But Kalora only smiled.)
williams: "But the Skin Horse only smiled."
```

**Notes:** the Skin Horse places love inside `sui ru laeno thimu`, for a very long time. Its purpose is not merely play, and the final `ru lothea` carries Williams's emphatic "REALLY loves you." The Rabbit's questions come from the body of a cloth toy: noisy machinery, a winding handle, and the suspicion that becoming Real may hurt. `welua miona`, fragile people, carries "people who break easily," while `se na thesa phelu` keeps careful handling distinct from care as a relationship. Williams gives the difficult claim to the Skin Horse: a Real toy accepts pain. Phi leaves it with him. `kiparu` names the joints, `ralu` lets them become loose, and `rohemi` describes wear rather than age. The answer concerns what matters to the toy, not a new claim about worth. Nearly all the hair has gone `lue lothea`, from love.
