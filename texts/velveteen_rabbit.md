# wuloe wetha tupiwa — The Velveteen Rabbit

*Margery Williams published The Velveteen Rabbit in 1922. A cloth rabbit is loved threadbare by a child, condemned to the bonfire after a fever, and carried at last by nursery magic into the life of a wild rabbit. This translation follows the complete story. Williams's capitalised Real becomes `shewo`, true, while names and exact source-bound terms remain in her lines beside the Phi. The result has room for the mouse, the ants, the sawdust heart, and one rather consequential tear.*

*Each block carries the Phi sentence, its word-by-word gloss, a literal English back-translation, and Williams's original wording. The source line sets the terms. Where Phi must describe rather than name a clock unit, title, institution, or object, the translation limits at the end keep the remaining distance in view.*

---

## mioru thorui — The splendid beginning

```
ta wuloe wetha tupiwa to nai.
one soft cloth rabbit PST be.
(There was once a velveteen rabbit.)
williams: "HERE was once a velveteen rabbit,"

mua thorui shia ru mioru to nai.

(In the beginning he was very beautiful.)
williams: "and in the beginning he was really splendid."

shia theru nela wuloe to nai.

(He was plump and soft.)
williams: "He was fat and bunchy,"

tupiwa theru nela wuloe na nai.

(A rabbit must be plump and soft.)
williams: "as a rabbit should be;"

shia nolika whila lo mureli piloe nela lo whilo piloe to phelu.
3SG animal hair PL brown spot COORD PL white spot PST hold.
(His fur held brown spots and white spots.)
williams: "his coat was spotted brown and white,"

shia menoa whila shewo sima to nai.

(His whiskers were real thread.)
williams: "he had real thread whiskers,"

mua shia shonui horathe welisha wetha to nai.

shila holia kelua to nai.

shia mua lopia paloi wethalu thorui to se peki.

shia ru mioru to nai.
3SG INTS beautiful PST be.
(He was altogether charming.)
williams: "the effect was charming."

mua wethalu we phirae thena to nai.
LOC garment ALSO different thing PST be.
(There were other things in the stocking too.)
williams: "There were other things in the stocking,"

lo kerou lureko nela lo thero welisha lureko to nai.
PL stone fruit COORD PL fire color fruit PST be.
(There were nuts, and fire-colored fruits.)
williams: "nuts and oranges"

ta nurako keli wilemo to nai.
one railway device toy PST be.
(There was a toy railway engine.)
williams: "and a toy engine,"

lo mureli kaelo kerou lureko nela ta keli misuki to nai.
PL brown sweet stone fruit COORD one device mouse PST be.
(There were brown sweet almonds, and a wind-up mouse.)
williams: "and chocolate almonds and a clockwork mouse,"

whekai tupiwa mo ko welao to nai.
CONTR rabbit CMPR FOC good PST be.
(But the rabbit was the best of all.)
williams: "but the Rabbit was quite the best of all."

lopia ta laeno shemu shia to lothea.
child one long moment 3SG PST love.
(For one long while, the child loved him.)
williams: "For at least two hours the Boy loved him,"

pheo thena lo thaluni kau nuola to shua.
POST thing PL kin ALL eat PST come.
(After that, the kinfolk came to the meal.)
williams: "and then Aunts and Uncles came to dinner,"

ta whalo pelua kohura to nai.
one large paper noise PST be.
(There was a great noise of paper.)
williams: "and there was a great rustling of tissue paper"

lo loami to se phae.
PL gift PST PASS open.
(The gifts were being opened.)
williams: "and unwrapping of parcels,"

lo miona theula newu loami to rashowe nila.
PL person UNIV new gift PST excited see.
(The people looked excitedly at all the new gifts.)
williams: "and in the excitement of looking at all the new presents"

wuloe wetha tupiwa to se sahu.
soft cloth rabbit PST PASS forget.
(The velveteen rabbit was forgotten.)
williams: "the Velveteen Rabbit was forgotten."
```

**Notes:** `ru mioru`, very beautiful, carries both "really splendid" and the later charming effect without inventing light around the rabbit. He is `theru nela wuloe`, plump and soft. Williams's "as a rabbit should be" gets a sentence of its own, complete with the obligation particle `na`, as though rabbit shape were a small law of nature. `piloe` (spot) describes the coat here and returns in the last scene, when those odd marks almost give the rabbit away. The stocking is `lopia paloi wethalu`, a child's foot-garment, and its open top is `thorui`. `se peki` says that the rabbit was squeezed into it. Holly becomes `shila liro loremi`, a branch of winter green. Canon does not count clock hours, so the child's "at least two hours" becomes `ta laeno shemu`, one long moment; the exact duration stays in Williams's line. The joke survives: the love is real, and not terribly durable. Aunts and Uncles become `lo thaluni`, kinfolk, because Phi leaves gender out of general kin terms. The stocking is already crowded. Stone-fruit nuts and fire-coloured oranges sit beside a little railway device. Brown sweet almonds keep company with `keli misuki`, the clockwork mouse. The passive `se sahu` closes the scene without assigning a culprit: the rabbit was forgotten.

```

DUR long time 3SG LOC toy container DISJ ON child space floor PST live.

williams: "The mechanical toys were very superior,"

lo shia theula phirae wilemo to thiku nila.

williams: "and looked down upon every one else;"

lo shia sheloi newu remo to phelu.

williams: "they were full of modern ideas,"

lo shia phea lo shewo wilemo to ro phoa.

(They kept acting as though they were Real toys.)
williams: "and pretended they were real."

wilemo luphonu sui wi meluna to ki liona.

(The toy boat had lived through two seasons.)
williams: "The model boat, who had lived through two seasons"

pai theula shia kolai to ki lepa.
NEAR UNIV 3SG pigment PST PFV fall.
(Nearly all his paint had fallen away.)
williams: "and lost most of his paint,"

shia lue lo shia ha keno to howela.
3SG ABL PL 3SG PROX custom PST receive.
(He caught this custom from them.)
williams: "caught the tone from them"

shia sheloi shemu roe theko haluma shia suro nela phora to therua thelima.
3SG MANY moment INS craft language 3SG rope COORD sail PST eager describe.
(He often and eagerly described his ropes and sails in craft language.)
williams: "and never missed an opportunity of referring to his rigging in technical terms."

lao shia tha lo shewo tupiwa to liona tho to ma sano tupiwa tha miso wheli thena reteru nai tho to po ma sherewa.
BECAUSE 3SG DECL.COMP PL true rabbit PST live DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST NEG know rabbit DECL.COMP REFL ANY thing model be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST POT NEG claim.
(Because he did not know that real rabbits lived, the rabbit could not claim to be a model of anything.)
williams: "The Rabbit could not claim to be a model of anything, for he didn't know that real rabbits existed;"

shia tha theula tupiwa phea miso roe wolea muhena se pilewa tho to remo.
3SG DECL.COMP UNIV rabbit AS REFL INS wood dust PASS make DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST think.
(He thought all rabbits, like himself, were made with wood-dust.)
williams: "he thought they were all stuffed with sawdust like himself,"

shia tha wolea muhena nosa serao thena nai tho to shelomu.
3SG DECL.COMP wood dust now old thing be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST understand.
(And he understood that wood-dust was now an old thing.)
williams: "and he understood that sawdust was quite out-of-date"

miona mawha thimu thoa lo newu remo miona wolea muhena na ma haolu.
person NONE time AMONG PL new think person wood dust NEC NEG speak.
(Among the new-thought people, one must never speak of wood-dust.)
williams: "and should never be mentioned in modern circles."

we shia whu lo kiparu phelu wolea whalo misheko to nai.
ALSO 3SG REL PL joint hold wood large cat PST be.
(Even the wooden great-cat who had joints was there.)
williams: "Even Timothy, the jointed wooden lion,"

lo sowelu miona shia to pilewa. lo shia whu roe puro phoa sholei wemari to nai.
PL disabled person 3SG PST make. PL 3SG REL INS strength do gather member PST be.
(Disabled people had made him. They were members of a group that acted through force.)
williams: "who was made by the disabled soldiers,"

thelao shia mo whalo remo to na phelu.
CONS 3SG CMPR large think PST NEC hold.
(So he ought to have held broader thoughts.)
williams: "and should have had broader views,"

whekai shia thoru to kelu. shia phea punoa kowanu punoki wemari to ro phoa.
CONTR 3SG proud PST become. 3SG AS society governance institution member PST HAB do.
(But he put on airs and kept acting as though he belonged to an institution of society's governance.)
williams: "put on airs and pretended he was connected with Government."

tupiwa tha lo phirae wilemo tupiwa li thiku sone tho to phaelo. shia thoa lo shia li ta wilemo to nai.
rabbit DECL.COMP PL different toy rabbit RESTR small value DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST feel. 3SG AMONG PL 3SG RESTR one toy PST be.
(The Rabbit felt that the other toys valued him only a little. He was merely one toy among the rest.)
williams: "Between them all the poor little Rabbit was made to feel himself very insignificant and commonplace,"

li kori kalora wei shia phena to nai.
RESTR leather horse DAT 3SG kind PST be.
(Only the Leather Horse was kind to him.)
williams: "and the only person who was kind to him at all was the Skin Horse."
```

**Notes:** the expensive toys are `whu mo thena wisola phelona wilemo`, toys that require more things in exchange. Price stays outside the lexicon, but the material relation does not disappear with it. Their snub is an act of valuation: the Rabbit feels that the other toys value him only a little. The next sentence leaves him one toy among the rest, where Williams can call him "commonplace" in a single word. The mechanical toys act like Real toys. `phea ... phoa` catches their performance; `peshu` would accuse them of knowing deception. The model boat still has its ropes, sails, craft language, and two seasons. `reteru` lets the rabbit deny being a model without turning a model into a mere symbol. The wooden great-cat has visible joints, `lo kiparu`. His makers are disabled people and members of a group that acts through force; Williams's line keeps their exact military role. Timothy likewise acts like a member of `punoa kowanu punoki`, an institution of society's governance. His airs do the rest.

## kori kalora — The Skin Horse

```
kori kalora sheo theula phirae wilemo mua lopia tholu mo laeno to ki liona.
leather horse THAN UNIV different toy LOC child space CMPR long PST PFV live.
(The Leather Horse had lived in the nursery longer than all the other toys.)
williams: "The Skin Horse had lived longer in the nursery than any of the others."

shia ru serao to nai.
3SG INTS old PST be.
(He was very old.)
williams: "He was so old"

shia mureli nolika whila mua soli lokue to ki wepu.
3SG brown animal hair LOC SOME place PST PFV go.
(In places, his brown fur had gone.)
williams: "that his brown coat was bald in patches"

phou whila lo pina to se nila.
BELOW hair PL sew PST PASS see.
(Beneath the hair, the sewing showed.)
williams: "and showed the seams underneath,"

pai theula shia tulira whila wei nolue kerou tiwa to ki se natu.
NEAR UNIV 3SG tail hair DAT neck stone tie PST PFV PASS pull.
(Nearly all his tail-hairs had been pulled out for the tying of neck-stones.)
williams: "and most of the hairs in his tail had been pulled out to string bead necklaces."

shia phue to nai.
3SG wise PST be.
(He was wise.)
williams: "He was wise,"

shia tha sheloi keli wilemo shua tho to ki nila.
3SG DECL.COMP MANY device toy come DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST PFV see.
(He had seen many mechanical toys come.)
williams: "for he had seen a long succession of mechanical toys arrive"

lo shia to thoru haolu. lo shia to thoru thalo.
PL 3SG PST proud speak. PL 3SG PST proud walk.
(They spoke proudly and walked with a swagger.)
williams: "to boast and swagger,"

pheo soli thimu lo shia keli korua to pukate.
POST SOME time PL 3SG device heart PST break.
(After a while, their device-hearts broke.)
williams: "and by-and-by break their mainsprings"

lo shia to lumeo.
PL 3SG PST die.
(And they passed away.)
williams: "and pass away,"

thelao shia tha lo shia li wilemo nai tho to sano.
CONS 3SG DECL.COMP PL 3SG RESTR toy be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST know.
(So he knew that they were only toys.)
williams: "and he knew that they were only toys,"

lo shia mawha thimu phirae thena so kelu.
PL 3SG NONE time different thing FUT become.
(They would never become anything else.)
williams: "and would never turn into anything else."

lopia tholu whekelu ru kuewa nela ru waora nai.
child space magic INTS strange COORD INTS wondrous be.
(For nursery magic is very strange and very wondrous.)
williams: "For nursery magic is very strange and wonderful,"

li lo whu serao nela phue nela seroli nai wilemo phea kori kalora shia shelomu.
RESTR PL REL old COORD wise COORD mature be toy AS leather horse 3SG understand.
(Only the toys that are old and wise and seasoned, like the Leather Horse, understand it.)
williams: "and only those playthings that are old and wise and experienced like the Skin Horse understand all about it."
```

**Notes:** the mainspring is `keli korua`, the device's heart. It is the part the mechanical toys boast about and the part they break, so `pukate` (break) begins its work before the story asks what love can do to a sawdust heart. Their final "pass away" is `lumeo`, die; Phi can say death gently without hiding it. Seams are `lo pina`, visible sewing, and bead necklaces are `nolue kerou`, neck-stones paid for with the lion's own tail. The section's rule has no tense: `lopia tholu whekelu ru kuewa nela ru waora nai`, nursery magic is very strange and very wondrous. The sound of `whekelu` recalls `kelu` (become), but the resemblance is a mnemonic, not a hidden derivation. The old horse is also `seroli`, mature, so experience has the ripeness Williams needs.

## shewo — What is Real

```
phoe lila tholu hiso ka kelu tholu woru shua lo shia ta philo sio wiso pai moeli to ruemi. tupiwa sha hina shewo nai sho to thilou.
ANT PURP space clean CAUS become space keeper come PL 3SG one day BESIDE RECP NEAR hearth PST lie down. rabbit QUOT.COMP what true be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(One day they lay beside each other near the hearth, before the keeper came to make the room clean. The rabbit asked: "What is Real?")
williams: "\"What is REAL?\" asked the Rabbit one day, when they were lying side by side near the nursery fender, before Nana came to tidy the room."

tupiwa sha wa mua thia lo whu kohura pilewa thena nai. wa ta keli manuwe mue thia weloni whano sho to thilou.
rabbit QUOT.COMP Q LOC 2SG PL REL noise make thing be. Q one device hand OUT.OF 2SG body stand QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(He asked: "Are there things inside you that make a noise? Does a device-hand stand out of your body?")
williams: "\"Does it mean having things that buzz inside you and a stick-out handle?\""

kori kalora sha shewo lue pilewa ma shua sho to haolu.
leather horse QUOT.COMP true ABL make NEG come QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(The Leather Horse said: "Real does not come from how you are made.")
williams: "\"Real isn't how you are made,\" said the Skin Horse."

sha shewo whu wei thia kelu thena nai sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP true REL DAT 2SG become thing be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("Real is a thing that happens to you.")
williams: "\"It's a thing that happens to you."

sha lu lopia sui ru laeno thimu thia lothea nela shia thia lothea li wile porua ma phelu nela shia thia ru lothea. pheo thena thia shewo kelu sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP COND child DUR INTS long time 2SG love COORD 3SG 2SG love RESTR play purpose NEG hold COORD 3SG 2SG INTS love. POST thing 2SG true become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("When a child loves you for a very long time, their love for you does not have mere play as its purpose, and they really love you, then you become Real.")
williams: "When a child loves you for a long, long time,"
williams: "not just to play with, but REALLY loves you,"
williams: "then you become Real.\""

tupiwa sha wa ha kipona thola sho to thilou.
rabbit QUOT.COMP Q PROX pain bring QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(The rabbit asked: "Does this bring pain?")
williams: "\"Does it hurt?\" asked the Rabbit."

kori kalora sha soli shemu sho to haolu.
leather horse QUOT.COMP SOME moment QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(The Leather Horse said: "Sometimes.")
williams: "\"Sometimes,\" said the Skin Horse,"

shia theula thimu wero to nai.
3SG UNIV time honest PST be.
(He was always honest.)
williams: "for he was always truthful."

sha lu thia shewo nai. thia kipona kelomi sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP COND 2SG true be. 2SG pain accept QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("When you are Real, you accept pain.")
williams: "\"When you are Real you don't mind being hurt.\""

shia sha wa ha mua ta shemu kelu phea keli rato sola ha roe lo thiku helui kelu sho to thilou.
3SG QUOT.COMP Q PROX LOC one moment become AS device turn DISJ PROX INS PL small change become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST inquire.
(He asked: "Does it happen in one moment, like the winding of a device, or by small changes?")
williams: "\"Does it happen all at once, like being wound up,\" he asked, \"or bit by bit?\""

kori kalora sha ha mua ta shemu ma kelu sho to haolu.
leather horse QUOT.COMP PROX LOC one moment NEG become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(The Leather Horse said: "It does not happen in one moment.")
williams: "\"It doesn't happen all at once,\" said the Skin Horse."

sha thia kelu. ha laeno thimu phelona sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP 2SG become. PROX long time require QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("You become. It requires a long time.")
williams: "\"You become. It takes a long time."

sha thelao ha wei lo welua miona phina shemu kelu sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP CONS PROX DAT PL fragile person FEW moment become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("That is why it comes only rarely to fragile people.")
williams: "That's why it doesn't happen often to people who break easily,"

sha ha wei lo whu tiso shuna phelu miona nela lo whu se na thesa phelu miona we phina shemu kelu sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP PROX DAT PL REL sharp edge hold person COORD PL REL PASS NEC careful hold person ALSO FEW moment become QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("To those who hold sharp edges, and those who must be carefully kept, it also comes rarely.")
williams: "or have sharp edges, or who have to be carefully kept."

sha lu thia shewo kelu. pai theula thia whila lue lothea ki wepu sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP COND 2SG true become. NEAR UNIV 2SG hair ABL love PFV go QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("By the time you become Real, nearly all your hair has been loved away.")
williams: "Generally, by the time you are Real, most of your hair has been loved off,"

sha thia mirae lepa. thia lo kiparu ralu kelu. thia ru rohemi sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP 2SG eye fall. 2SG PL joint free become. 2SG INTS wear down QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("Your eyes fall out. Your joints grow loose. You become very worn.")
williams: "and your eyes drop out and you get loose in the joints and very shabby."

sha whekai thia ha lo thena ma sone sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP CONTR 2SG PROX PL thing NEG value QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("But you do not care about these things.")
williams: "But these things don't matter at all,"

sha lu thia shewo kelu. li whu ma shelomu miona thia ma mioru nila sho to haolu.
QUOT.COMP COND 2SG true become. RESTR REL NEG understand person 2SG NEG beautiful see QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
("Once you become Real, only the people who do not understand see you as unbeautiful.")
williams: "because once you are Real you can't be ugly, except to people who don't understand.\""

tupiwa sha thia shewo ho nai sho to haolu.
rabbit QUOT.COMP 2SG true ASSUM be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(The rabbit said: "You are Real, I suppose?")
williams: "\"I suppose you are real?\" said the Rabbit."

lao shia tha kori kalora korua phaeli po nai tho to remo pheo thena shia tha miso ha to ma haolu tho to pula.
BECAUSE 3SG DECL.COMP leather horse heart tender POT be DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST think POST thing 3SG DECL.COMP REFL PROX PST NEG speak DECL.COMP.CLOSE PST wish.
(Because he thought the Leather Horse might have a tender heart, he wished that he had not said it.)
williams: "And then he wished he had not said it, for he thought the Skin Horse might be sensitive."

whekai kori kalora li to seniku.
CONTR leather horse RESTR PST smile.
(But the Leather Horse only smiled.)
williams: "But the Skin Horse only smiled."
```

**Notes:** the Skin Horse places love inside `sui ru laeno thimu`, for a very long time. Its purpose is not merely play, and the final `ru lothea` restores Williams's emphatic "REALLY loves you." The Rabbit's questions still come from the body of a cloth toy: noisy machinery, a winding handle, and the suspicion that becoming Real may hurt. `welua miona`, fragile people, carries "people who break easily," while `se na thesa phelu` keeps careful handling distinct from care as a relationship. Williams gives the difficult claim to the Skin Horse: a Real toy accepts pain. Phi leaves it with him. `kiparu` names the joints, `ralu` lets them become loose, and `rohemi` describes wear rather than age. The later answer concerns what matters to the toy, not a new claim about worth. Nearly all the hair has gone `lue lothea`, from love.

For them, however, the shape can change. `wiloru` states dizziness directly, and `tiphori` supplies the body-wide tickly feeling. The feeling is `kuewa`, strange, rather than amusing; `wheli muila thena` then restores anything in the world. The stranger wrinkles the skin of his nose with `pakiro lupaki` and draws his ears back before he jumps away. His complaint concerns a scent that is not `theali`, fitting; `telua` (accurate) belongs to representations and was never at home in a rabbit's nose. The pincushion remains `tisu molu`, a needle-holder, while `ke` marks conclusions drawn from the invisible seams. The taunts remain taunts. They include the laugh and "A rabbit without any hind legs!" Phi reports their astonishment without teaching contempt as a virtue.
