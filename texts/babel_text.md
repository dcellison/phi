# ta haluma — The Babel Text

*A constructed language is scarcely introduced before somebody asks for its Babel text. Genesis 11:1-9 tells how one speech became many, and Jeffrey Henning proposed it as a standard conlang text in 1996. Phi uses the King James Version, the customary reference for the exercise, as its ground truth. Its wording is stored in `texts/sources/kjv_genesis.txt` and cited below in full.*

*The translation keeps the builders' fear, their tower, the loss of mutual understanding, and their dispersal across the earth. It cannot carry LORD, Shinar, or Babel as Phi forms without inventing names or admitting a title Phi refuses. Those identities remain exact in the adjacent King James lines, while the Phi describes the authority, places, and events that matter to the story.*

*Each aligned unit shows the Phi, an exact structural gloss, English derived from the frozen Phi alone, and the corresponding King James wording. The complete Phi reading and a table of translation limits follow.*

---

## Translation

The story turns on command and consequence. Nine aligned units carry its nine verses: verse 3 takes two units for its two sentences, while the LORD's continuing speech across verses 6 and 7 stays inside one quote frame. The two accounts of the scattering remain two accounts; the last is not discarded as a biblical habit of repeating oneself.

### One language

```
theula muila ta haluma nela ta haolu to phelu.
[UNIV earth] [[one language] COORD [one speak]] PST hold.
(The whole earth had one language and one speech.)
kjv: "And the whole earth was of one language, and of one speech."

whau lo miona lue sileta thorui to laniru lo shia mua muila ta wemo to hekawi. lo shia mua ha lokue to menui.
[SIMUL [[PL person] [ABL [sun beginning]] PST journey] [[PL 3SG] [LOC earth] [one plain] PST find]]. [PL 3SG] [LOC [PROX place]] PST dwell.
(While people journeyed from the east, they found a plain on the earth. They lived in this place.)
kjv: "And it came to pass, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there."
```

**Notes:** `ta haluma nela ta haolu` gives the opening both a shared language and one speech. `whau` places the discovery within the journey instead of turning them into unrelated events, and `sileta thorui`, the sun's beginning, supplies east. The plain lies in a land, but the Phi does not pretend to know that land by the source name Shinar.

### Brick and tower

```
lo shia wei wiso sha su lo mia lo mueri kerou pilewa. su lo mia lo mueri kerou ru thape sho to haolu.
[PL 3SG] [DAT RECP] [QUOT.COMP [OPT [PL 1SG] [PL [clay stone]] make]. [OPT [PL 1SG] [PL [clay stone]] [INTS burn]] QUOT.COMP.CLOSE] PST speak.
(They said to one another, "May we make bricks. May we fire the bricks thoroughly.")
kjv: "And they said one to another, Go to, let us make brick, and burn them thoroughly."

lo mueri kerou wei lo miona kerou to nai. theru nuko loesi wei lo miona lo mueri kerou lorea muralo to nai.
[PL [clay stone]] [DAT [PL person]] stone PST be. [thick [black oil]] [DAT [PL person]] [[PL [clay stone]] connect material] PST be.
(The bricks were stone for the people. For them, thick black oil was material for connecting the bricks.)
kjv: "And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter."

lo shia sha su lila lo mia nia theula muila leko so se ma thiwera lo mia wei lo mia whalo silawo nela torali lorima. torali raeli lumae mua waero po kamo. su lo mia nomei wei sheloi miona se sano sho to haolu.
[PL 3SG] [QUOT.COMP [OPT [PURP [PL 1SG] [ON [[UNIV earth] surface]] FUT PASS NEG scatter] [PL 1SG] [DAT [PL 1SG]] [[large village] COORD tower] build]. [[tower height] end] [LOC sky] POT arrive. [OPT [[PL 1SG] name] [DAT [MANY person]] PASS know] QUOT.COMP.CLOSE] PST speak.
(They said, "May we build ourselves a large village and a tower so that we will not be scattered across the whole surface of the earth. The tower's top may reach the sky. May our name be known to many people.")
kjv: "And they said, Go to, let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven; and let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth."
```

**Notes:** The archaic "Go to" joins the exhortation rather than sending the builders anywhere, so `su` carries it. The registered `mueri kerou`, clay-stone, names brick. `theru nuko loesi`, thick black oil, describes bitumen, while `lo mueri kerou lorea muralo` says what it does as mortar. The city is `whalo silawo`, a large village, and Work-module `torali` names the tower. A separate sentence gives `torali raeli lumae`, the end of the tower's height, its possible reach into the sky. Purpose-first `lila` places the feared dispersal before the building intended to prevent it; another optative sentence asks that the builders' name become known.

### Descent and judgment

```
ta limoe karami to phelu. lo miona lo lumira whalo silawo nela torali to lorima. lila shia whalo silawo nela torali nila shia lue waero kau muila to shua.
[one creature] authority PST hold. [[PL person] [PL descendant]] [[large village] COORD tower] PST build. [PURP [3SG [[large village] COORD tower] see] 3SG [ABL sky] [ALL earth] PST come].
(One creature held authority. The people's descendants built a large village and a tower. The creature came from the sky to the earth to see the large village and tower.)
kjv: "And the LORD came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men builded."

shia sha no nila. theula miona ta punoa nai. lo shia ta haluma phelu. lo shia ha pa phoa. lo shia tha lo shia phoa tho ki weni. nosa mawha phoa wei lo shia noraku so kelu. su lo mia lue waero kau muila wepu. su lila lo shia lue wiso haolu po ma shelomu lo mia mua ha lokue lo shia ta haluma lo phirae haluma ka kelu sho to haolu.
[3SG [QUOT.COMP [IMP see]. [[UNIV person] [one society] be]. [[PL 3SG] [one language] hold]. [[PL 3SG] PROX INCH do]. [[PL 3SG] [DECL.COMP [PL 3SG] do DECL.COMP.CLOSE] PFV imagine]. [now [NONE do] [DAT [PL 3SG]] blocked FUT become]. [OPT [PL 1SG] [ABL sky] [ALL earth] go]. [OPT [PURP [[PL 3SG] [ABL RECP] speak POT NEG understand] [[PL 1SG] [LOC [PROX place]] [[PL 3SG] [one language]] [PL [different language]] CAUS become]] QUOT.COMP.CLOSE] PST speak].
(The creature said, "Look. All people are one society. They have one language. They are beginning to do this. They have imagined themselves acting. Now no action will become blocked for them. May we go from the sky to the earth. May we turn their one language into different languages here, so that they cannot understand one another's speech.")
kjv: "And the LORD said, Behold, the people is one, and they have all one language; and this they begin to do: and now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do."
kjv: "Go to, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another's speech."
```

**Notes:** The exact title LORD stays in the source. Phi first establishes `ta limoe karami to phelu`, one creature held authority, then uses `shia` for that story participant. The descendants are not recast as young children. Source and destination carry the downward journey, while `lila` puts seeing before the journey undertaken for it. One quote frame holds the complete continuing speech. The builders have imagined acting, and no action will become `noraku`, blocked. In the last optative, one language becomes different languages for the stated purpose of preventing reciprocal understanding.

### Confounding and dispersal

```
thelao shia lue ha lokue nia theula muila leko lo miona to thiwera nela lo miona whalo silawo to te lorima.
[CONS [3SG [ABL [PROX place]] [ON [[UNIV earth] surface]] [PL person] PST scatter] COORD [[PL person] [large village] PST CESS build]].
(Therefore, the creature scattered the people from this place across the whole surface of the earth, and the people stopped building the large village.)
kjv: "So the LORD scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off to build the city."

shia mua ha lokue theula muila ta haluma lo phirae haluma to ka kelu. thelao nomei wei whalo silawo se loa. sheno shia lue ha lokue nia theula muila leko lo miona to thiwera.
[3SG [LOC [PROX place]] [[UNIV earth] [one language]] [PL [different language]] PST CAUS become]. [CONS name [DAT [large village]] PASS give]. [ADD 3SG [ABL [PROX place]] [ON [[UNIV earth] surface]] [PL person] PST scatter].
(At this place, the creature turned the one language of the whole earth into different languages. Therefore, the large village is given a name. The creature also scattered the people from this place across the whole surface of the earth.)
kjv: "Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the LORD did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did the LORD scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth."
```

**Notes:** Phi unpacks confounding into a change from one language to different languages and the intended loss of reciprocal understanding. `thiwera` reports the two dispersals directly, and `nia theula muila leko` keeps them on the earth's surface without borrowing the human body-part `menoa`, face. In the last unit, the change comes first so that `thelao` can mark the naming as its consequence. `sheno` then adds the repeated scattering without placing it inside that naming inference. Babel remains in the citation because the source city has no accepted Phi-form onym.

## Complete translation

```
ta haluma

theula muila ta haluma nela ta haolu to phelu. whau lo miona lue sileta thorui to laniru lo shia mua muila ta wemo to hekawi. lo shia mua ha lokue to menui.

lo shia wei wiso sha su lo mia lo mueri kerou pilewa. su lo mia lo mueri kerou ru thape sho to haolu. lo mueri kerou wei lo miona kerou to nai. theru nuko loesi wei lo miona lo mueri kerou lorea muralo to nai.

lo shia sha su lila lo mia nia theula muila leko so se ma thiwera lo mia wei lo mia whalo silawo nela torali lorima. torali raeli lumae mua waero po kamo. su lo mia nomei wei sheloi miona se sano sho to haolu.

ta limoe karami to phelu. lo miona lo lumira whalo silawo nela torali to lorima. lila shia whalo silawo nela torali nila shia lue waero kau muila to shua. shia sha no nila. theula miona ta punoa nai. lo shia ta haluma phelu. lo shia ha pa phoa. lo shia tha lo shia phoa tho ki weni. nosa mawha phoa wei lo shia noraku so kelu. su lo mia lue waero kau muila wepu. su lila lo shia lue wiso haolu po ma shelomu lo mia mua ha lokue lo shia ta haluma lo phirae haluma ka kelu sho to haolu.

thelao shia lue ha lokue nia theula muila leko lo miona to thiwera nela lo miona whalo silawo to te lorima. shia mua ha lokue theula muila ta haluma lo phirae haluma to ka kelu. thelao nomei wei whalo silawo se loa. sheno shia lue ha lokue nia theula muila leko lo miona to thiwera.
```

## Translation limits

| King James wording | Phi rendering | Remaining difference |
|---|---|---|
| one language and one speech | `ta haluma nela ta haolu` | `haluma` names the shared system; the event noun of speaking gives the second term a wider range than the King James idiom. |
| Shinar | a plain in the land, with Shinar kept in the source line | The place relation remains, but the Phi passage alone does not identify the land. |
| Go to, let us | optative `su` | The optative carries the exhortation without giving the archaic "Go to" a false motion sense. |
| brick, slime, mortar | clay-stone and thick black oil as brick-connecting material | Brick and the mortar's work are explicit. `theru nuko loesi` approaches bitumen through appearance and mineral-oil kinship rather than naming asphalt. |
| city and tower | `whalo silawo`, large village, and `torali`, tower | Phi keeps the settlement at human scale through a transparent phrase. The Work root names the tower directly, and `torali raeli lumae` gives its top as the end of its height. |
| make us a name | may our name be known to many people | Phi states the desired recognition rather than copying the English idiom as if a name were manufactured. |
| lest we be scattered | so that we will not be scattered | Purpose-first order places the feared result before the building meant to prevent it. |
| the LORD | `ta limoe karami to phelu`, one creature held authority | The source title stays outside Phi. `karami` reports authority without settling its legitimacy, while `limoe` places the unknown actor beyond ordinary human reference. |
| came down | came from the sky to the earth | Source and destination together carry downward motion without a dedicated verb for descent. |
| children of men | descendants of people | Lineage remains, while the masculine expression and its idiomatic sense of humankind do not. |
| nothing will be restrained from them | no action will become blocked for them | `noraku` means unable to proceed because of an obstacle, not restraint by an unnamed agent. |
| confound their language | cause one language to become different languages | The change is stated directly, and its purpose clause gives the intended loss of reciprocal understanding. |
| face of the earth | `nia theula muila leko`, across the whole earth's surface | Phi uses its ordinary noun for a physical surface rather than extending the body-part `menoa`, face, to land. |
| scattered abroad | scattered from the place across the earth's surface | `thiwera` states dispersal without judging it welcome; the authority established for the actor supplies the punitive force. |
| Babel | a name is given, with Babel kept in the source line | The naming event and its cause remain; the exact source name and Hebrew wordplay do not enter the Phi passage. |
