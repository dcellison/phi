# *Kahlil Gibran*

Gibran's speaker has scarcely finished with love when children and giving ask for their turn. Phi keeps these neighbouring teachings together under *The Prophet*, while leaving room for other writing by Gibran to have pages of its own. The first page holds three selections, with On Children rendered both closely and through Phi's five pillars; the complete [source witness](sources/the_prophet.txt) sits beside it.
