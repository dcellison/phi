# *Kahlil Gibran*

Gibran's Almustafa remains a prophet in the 1923 source. The collection title `phewo phelui`, the sage's words, names what the figure does in these pages without erasing Gibran's office. Love, children, giving, and work have a page apiece in complete Phi translation. Together they begin a translation of the whole of *The Prophet*. The complete [source witness](sources/the_prophet.txt) sits beside them. On Giving introduces `parelu`, deserve, and `thaweno`, reward, so that deservingness does not have to borrow inherent worth and a reward does not have to masquerade as a gift.
