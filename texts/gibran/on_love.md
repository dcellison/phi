# lothea — On Love

*Almitra asks about love, and Gibran answers with wings that hide a sword and a garden caught by the north wind. Later, grain passes under the millstone and bread enters sacred fire. The Phi translation includes the whole teaching, wounds and all.*

*Each passage uses a four-line block: the Phi sentence, its exact lexicon gloss, a literal English back-translation, and Gibran's source wording. A continuous Phi reading and a table of translation limits follow.*

---

## Close translation

This is the whole teaching, from Almitra's request to the prayer and song at night. The translation describes Gibran's sword and crown without adding roots for them. Crucifixion, threshing, and sacred feast receive the same treatment. The source remains beside every passage, where its names and inherited identities stay visible.

### The summons and the wings

```
ta himo miona sha no wei lo mia nia lothea haolu sho to haolu.
one HUM.CLF person QUOT.COMP IMP DAT PL 1SG ON love speak QUOT.COMP.CLOSE PST speak.
(A person said, "Speak to us about love.")
gibran: "Then said Almitra, Speak to us of _Love_."

phirae miona miso koma to ka rihe. shia lo miona to somela. lo miona maeli to kelu. shia roe theisa haoni to haolu.
different person REFL head PST CAUS rise. 3SG PL person PST observe. PL person quiet PST become. 3SG INS loud voice PST speak.
(A different person raised their head and observed the people. The people became quiet. They spoke with a loud voice.)
gibran: "And he raised his head and looked upon the people, and there fell a stillness upon them. And with a great voice he said:"

lu lothea thia whola. no lothea ruela thalo.
COND love 2SG invite. IMP love path walk.
(When love invites you, walk love's path.)
gibran: "When love beckons to you, follow him,"

whekai lo shia ruela kethua nai. lo shia ruela ratenu nai.
CONTR PL 3SG path hard be. PL 3SG path steep be.
(However, its paths are hard and steep.)
gibran: "Though his ways are hard and steep."

lu shia lo wapile thia lomare. no shia kelomi.
COND 3SG PL wing 2SG embrace. IMP 3SG accept.
(When its wings embrace you, accept it.)
gibran: "And when his wings enfold you yield to him,"

whekai [whu thoa shia lo wapile se kupe] tiso laeno keluo thena wei thia kaworu po loa.
CONTR [REL AMONG 3SG PL wing PASS hide] sharp long metal thing DAT 2SG injury POT give.
(However, a long sharp metal thing hidden among its wings may give you an injury.)
gibran: "Though the sword hidden among his pinions may wound you."
```

**Notes:** Almitra's name remains in the source line; Phi uses `ta himo miona`, one person, because an unadapted name cannot occupy a Phi sentence. The beckon is an invitation, and following it means walking love's path. With `ratenu`, the slope is steep without being fast. Yielding becomes acceptance of love itself. Phi has no neutral word for sword. Here it is a long sharp metal thing hidden among wings, and its possible wound is `kaworu`, an injury rather than decorative pain.

### Voice, crown, branch, and root

```
lu shia wei thia haolu. no shia theomi.
COND 3SG DAT 2SG speak. IMP 3SG trust.
(When it speaks to you, trust it.)
gibran: "And when he speaks to you believe in him,"

whekai shia haoni lo thia whemura ka po pukate. senao keiro nitho howeli thepalu ka po kema pukeri.
CONTR 3SG voice PL 2SG dream CAUS POT break. same way north wind garden CAUS POT strong damage.
(However, its voice may cause your dreams to break, just as the north wind may severely damage a garden.)
gibran: "Though his voice may shatter your dreams as the north wind lays waste the garden."

lothea roe [whu nia koma se wenuha] keluro thena thia so keluro. shia wi themo wolea so tiwa. senao keiro lothea nia ha wolea tei thia lumeo thia so phelu. lothea thia so ka thuroa. senao keiro shia lo thia loremi so kati.
love INS [REL ON head PASS wear] honor thing 2SG FUT honor. 3SG two THING.CLF wood FUT tie. same way love ON PROX wood UNTIL 2SG die 2SG FUT hold. love 2SG FUT CAUS grow. same way 3SG PL 2SG branch FUT cut.
(Love will honour you with a sign of honour worn on the head. It will tie two pieces of wood together. In the same way, love will hold you on this wood until your death. Love will cause you to grow, and it will also cut your branches.)
gibran: "For even as love crowns you so shall he crucify you. Even as he is for your growth so is he for your pruning."

shia kau thia raeli so kalei. shia lo [whu mua sileta phelo tiripe] mo ko phaeli loremi so moli palo.
3SG ALL 2SG height FUT climb. 3SG PL [REL LOC sun light quiver] CMPR FOC tender branch FUT gentle touch.
(It will climb to your height. It will gently touch your most tender branches, which quiver in sunlight.)
gibran: "Even as he ascends to your height and caresses your tenderest branches that quiver in the sun,"

shia lue thia raeli kau lo thia tumeki so wepu. lo thia tumeki muila kema phelu. shia lo thia tumeki so ka tiripe.
3SG ABL 2SG height ALL PL 2SG root FUT go. PL 2SG root earth strong hold. 3SG PL 2SG root FUT CAUS quiver.
(It will go from your height to your roots. Your roots hold the earth strongly. It will cause your roots to quiver.)
gibran: "So shall he descend to your roots and shake them in their clinging to the earth."

shia kau miso thia phea [whu roe nira se tiwa] napuro so sholei.
3SG ALL REFL 2SG AS [REL INS cord PASS tie] grain FUT gather.
(It will gather you to itself as grain tied with cord.)
gibran: "Like sheaves of corn he gathers you unto himself."
```

**Notes:** Gibran tells the listener to believe in a personified love, so Phi uses `theomi`, trust, rather than `nohero`, belief that a proposition is true. The north wind damages the garden severely; `whemori`, resource waste, does not belong to a ruined bed of plants. Crown and crucifixion are rebuilt in material steps. A crown is a head-worn sign of honour. Crucifixion ties two pieces of wood and leaves a body held there until death. Their symmetry remains, but neither object gains a reusable Phi name. Growth uses causative `thuroa`; pruning is the cutting of branches. Love then climbs, touches, descends, and makes the roots quiver while their hold on earth is visible.

### Grain, fire, and the heart

```
lila thia salenu kelu shia thia kema palo.
PURP 2SG unclothed become 3SG 2SG strong touch.
(To make you unclothed, it makes forceful contact with you.)
gibran: "He threshes you to make you naked."

lila shia lue thia lo [whu thia tawemi] thena leiro shia thue mera thia ka roke.
PURP 3SG ABL 2SG PL [REL 2SG cover] thing release 3SG THROUGH net 2SG CAUS move.
(To release from you the things that cover you, it moves you through a net.)
gibran: "He sifts you to free you from your husks."

shia muo whilo thia kuremi.
3SG INTO white 2SG grind.
(It grinds you into whiteness.)
gibran: "He grinds you to whiteness."

shia thia lupaki nela pesa. ha teru tei thia luwi manolu.
3SG 2SG fold COORD push. PROX process UNTIL 2SG flexible stay.
(It folds and presses you. This process continues until you are flexible.)
gibran: "He kneads you until you are pliant;"

lila thia wei thoepa phiora thoepa holia nurome thoepa napa kelu shia muo shia thoepa thero thia sepho.
PURP 2SG DAT sacred spirit sacred festival meal sacred bread become 3SG INTO 3SG sacred fire 2SG send.
(So that you may become sacred bread for the sacred spirit's sacred festival meal, it sends you into its sacred fire.)
gibran: "And then he assigns you to his sacred fire, that you may become sacred bread for God’s sacred feast."

lila thia thia korua lo kupela sano lothea ha theula thena wei thia so phoa. thue ha sano thia lioru korua thiku phanoi so kelu.
PURP 2SG 2SG heart PL secret know love PROX UNIV thing DAT 2SG FUT do. THROUGH PROX know 2SG life heart small portion FUT become.
(So that you may know the secrets of your heart, love will do all these things to you. Through this knowledge, you will become a small portion of Life's heart.)
gibran: "All these things shall love do unto you that you may know the secrets of your heart, and in that knowledge become a fragment of Life’s heart."
```

**Notes:** Phi has no thresh, sift, husk, or knead root, so each operation remains visible. Forceful contact leaves the person `salenu`, unclothed, while movement through a net releases what covered them. Grinding brings whiteness; folding and pressure continue to flexibility. Gibran's purpose must precede the action it explains, so the sacred bread comes before the sending into fire. `thoepa phiora`, sacred spirit, describes the divine referent while the source line preserves God. The event/result-noun rule lets `phanoi`, portion, name the small portion of Life's heart without requiring a word for a broken fragment.

### Fear and sufficient love

```
lu thia mua sukima li lothea shea nela li lothea nirelo sheraki. thelao wei thia mo welao nai. no roe wetha thia weloni tawemi. no lue lothea napuro riola tomae pholeni.
COND 2SG LOC fear RESTR love peace COORD RESTR love pleasant search. CONS DAT 2SG CMPR good be. IMP INS cloth 2SG body cover. IMP ABL love grain labor floor depart.
(If, in fear, you search only for love's peace and love's pleasure, then this is better for you: cover your body with cloth and depart from love's grain-working floor.)
gibran: "But if in your fear you would seek only love’s peace and love’s pleasure, Then it is better for you that you cover your nakedness and pass out of love’s threshing-floor,"

thia muo [whu mawha meluna phelu] lokue so koema. thia so hola. whekai thia hola whoa so ma nai. thia so nuhewa. whekai thia nuhewa whoa so ma nai.
2SG INTO [REL NONE season hold] place FUT enter. 2SG FUT laugh. CONTR 2SG laugh whole FUT NEG be. 2SG FUT weep. CONTR 2SG weep whole FUT NEG be.
(You will enter a place that holds no season. You will laugh, but your laughter will not be whole. You will weep, but your weeping will not be whole.)
gibran: "Into the seasonless world where you shall laugh, but not all of your laughter, and weep, but not all of your tears."

lothea li miso loa. lothea li lue miso pilu.
love RESTR REFL give. love RESTR ABL REFL take.
(Love gives only itself; love takes only from itself.)
gibran: "Love gives naught but itself and takes naught but from itself."

lothea ma phelu. lothea se ma phelu.
love NEG hold. love PASS NEG hold.
(Love holds nothing, and is not held.)
gibran: "Love possesses not nor would it be possessed;"

lothea wei lothea henoi nai.
love DAT love ENOUGH be.
(Love is enough for love.)
gibran: "For love is sufficient unto love."
```

**Notes:** The conditional preserves Gibran's judgment intact. `sheraki` gives seeking its own act, with love's peace and pleasure separately restricted. The instruction makes covering physical by putting cloth over the body, and the threshing-floor is a floor where grain work happens. The seasonless world holds no season; laughter and weeping still occur there, but neither is whole. The next three claims need no enlargement. Love gives itself, cannot be possessed, and is enough for love.

### Direction and desire

```
lu thia lothea. no sha thoepa phiora mua mia korua nai sho ma haolu. no sha mia mua thoepa phiora korua nai sho haolu.
COND 2SG love. IMP QUOT.COMP sacred spirit LOC 1SG heart be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE NEG speak. IMP QUOT.COMP 1SG LOC sacred spirit heart be QUOT.COMP.CLOSE speak.
(When you love, do not say, "The sacred spirit is in my heart." Say, "I am in the heart of the sacred spirit.")
gibran: "When you love you should not say, “God is in my heart,” but rather, “I am in the heart of God.”"

no tha thia lothea ruela po kulo tho ma remo. lu lothea tha thia lothea kulo parelu tho hekawi. lothea thia ruela kulo.
IMP DECL.COMP 2SG love path POT guide DECL.COMP.CLOSE NEG think. COND love DECL.COMP 2SG love guide deserve DECL.COMP.CLOSE find. love 2SG path guide.
(Do not think that you can guide love's path. If love finds that you deserve its guidance, love guides your path.)
gibran: "And think not you can direct the course of love, for love, if it finds you worthy, directs your course."

lothea li tha miso pheno kelu tho rinu.
love RESTR DECL.COMP REFL full become DECL.COMP.CLOSE want.
(Love wants only to become full.)
gibran: "Love has no other desire but to fulfil itself."

lu thia lothea nela lo rinu na phelu. su thia ha lo rinu phelu.
COND 2SG love COORD PL want NEC hold. OPT 2SG PROX PL want hold.
(If you love and must have desires, may these be the desires you hold.)
gibran: "But if you love and must needs have desires, let these be your desires:"

su thia lue patoku muo larewu kelu. su thia phea thiku luphore nai. ha luphore selu. shia wei shero miso melira meliho.
OPT 2SG ABL solid INTO liquid become. OPT 2SG AS small river be. PROX river flow. 3SG DAT night REFL song sing.
(May you become liquid from solid. May you be like a small river. This river flows and sings its own song to the night.)
gibran: "To melt and be like a running brook that sings its melody to the night."
```

**Notes:** Within Phi, God is a sacred spirit; Gibran's line still says God. `parelu` keeps deservingness apart from the inherent worth every person already has, and love's guidance supplies the outcome being judged deserved. Phi guides rather than directs, so the two paths preserve Gibran's reversal without a commander's hand. `rinu` gives the source its desire rather than a wished-for outcome; fullness supplies fulfilment. The modal `na` makes "must needs" audible before the optative names the desires to hold. Melting is a change from solid `patoku` to liquid `larewu`; the brook then receives its own flow and song.

### Tenderness and the natural day

```
su thia wo phaeli kipona sano.
OPT 2SG EXC tender pain know.
(May you know the pain of too much tenderness.)
gibran: "To know the pain of too much tenderness."

su thia lue miso lothea shelomu kaworu howela.
OPT 2SG ABL REFL love understand injury receive.
(May you receive an injury from your own understanding of love.)
gibran: "To be wounded by your own understanding of love;"

su thia roe kanu nela siora lorika leiro.
OPT 2SG INS choose COORD joy blood release.
(May you release blood by choice and with joy.)
gibran: "And to bleed willingly and joyfully."

su thia mua horathe roe wapile korua nowae. su lao we ta lothea philo thia sha mea sho haolu.
OPT 2SG LOC dawn INS wing heart awaken. OPT BECAUSE ALSO one love day 2SG QUOT.COMP thanks QUOT.COMP.CLOSE speak.
(May you awaken at dawn with a wing-heart, and say "thanks" for one more day of loving.)
gibran: "To wake at dawn with a winged heart and give thanks for another day of loving;"

su thia mua thaeso therilu. su thia mua lothea ru siora phirena.
OPT 2SG LOC midday rest. OPT 2SG LOC love INTS joy meditate.
(May you rest at midday, and meditate within love's intense joy.)
gibran: "To rest at the noon hour and meditate love’s ecstacy;"

su thia mua howai nemo kau womu turema.
OPT 2SG LOC evening grateful ALL home return.
(May you return home at evening with gratitude.)
gibran: "To return home at eventide with gratitude;"

su thia nulae. su mua thia korua wei [whu thia meropi] miona thorai nai. su nia thia lo phimei ta woraka melira nai.
OPT 2SG sleep. OPT LOC 2SG heart DAT [REL 2SG cherish] person bless be. OPT ON 2SG PL lip one appreciate song be.
(May you sleep, with a blessing in your heart for the person you cherish and a song of appreciation upon your lips.)
gibran: "And then to sleep with a prayer for the beloved in your heart and a song of praise upon your lips."
```

**Notes:** The close translation does not soften the final cost. Excessive tenderness has pain, understanding gives injury, and choice and joy accompany released blood. `wapile korua`, wing-heart, recalls the opening image when dawn returns. Phi's natural day supplies dawn, `thaeso` at midday, evening, and sleep without importing clock units. Intense joy renders ecstasy. Prayer and praise have no universal roots: a blessing for the cherished person rests in the heart, while a song of appreciation rests on the lips.

## Complete translation

```
ta himo miona sha no wei lo mia nia lothea haolu sho to haolu. phirae miona miso koma to ka rihe. shia lo miona to somela. lo miona maeli to kelu. shia roe theisa haoni to haolu.

lu lothea thia whola. no lothea ruela thalo. whekai lo shia ruela kethua nai. lo shia ruela ratenu nai. lu shia lo wapile thia lomare. no shia kelomi. whekai [whu thoa shia lo wapile se kupe] tiso laeno keluo thena wei thia kaworu po loa.

lu shia wei thia haolu. no shia theomi. whekai shia haoni lo thia whemura ka po pukate. senao keiro nitho howeli thepalu ka po kema pukeri. lothea roe [whu nia koma se wenuha] keluro thena thia so keluro. shia wi themo wolea so tiwa. senao keiro lothea nia ha wolea tei thia lumeo thia so phelu. lothea thia so ka thuroa. senao keiro shia lo thia loremi so kati. shia kau thia raeli so kalei. shia lo [whu mua sileta phelo tiripe] mo ko phaeli loremi so moli palo. shia lue thia raeli kau lo thia tumeki so wepu. lo thia tumeki muila kema phelu. shia lo thia tumeki so ka tiripe. shia kau miso thia phea [whu roe nira se tiwa] napuro so sholei.

lila thia salenu kelu shia thia kema palo. lila shia lue thia lo [whu thia tawemi] thena leiro shia thue mera thia ka roke. shia muo whilo thia kuremi. shia thia lupaki nela pesa. ha teru tei thia luwi manolu. lila thia wei thoepa phiora thoepa holia nurome thoepa napa kelu shia muo shia thoepa thero thia sepho. lila thia thia korua lo kupela sano lothea ha theula thena wei thia so phoa. thue ha sano thia lioru korua thiku phanoi so kelu.

lu thia mua sukima li lothea shea nela li lothea nirelo sheraki. thelao wei thia mo welao nai. no roe wetha thia weloni tawemi. no lue lothea napuro riola tomae pholeni. thia muo [whu mawha meluna phelu] lokue so koema. thia so hola. whekai thia hola whoa so ma nai. thia so nuhewa. whekai thia nuhewa whoa so ma nai. lothea li miso loa. lothea li lue miso pilu. lothea ma phelu. lothea se ma phelu. lothea wei lothea henoi nai.

lu thia lothea. no sha thoepa phiora mua mia korua nai sho ma haolu. no sha mia mua thoepa phiora korua nai sho haolu. no tha thia lothea ruela po kulo tho ma remo. lu lothea tha thia lothea kulo parelu tho hekawi. lothea thia ruela kulo. lothea li tha miso pheno kelu tho rinu. lu thia lothea nela lo rinu na phelu. su thia ha lo rinu phelu. su thia lue patoku muo larewu kelu. su thia phea thiku luphore nai. ha luphore selu. shia wei shero miso melira meliho.

su thia wo phaeli kipona sano. su thia lue miso lothea shelomu kaworu howela. su thia roe kanu nela siora lorika leiro. su thia mua horathe roe wapile korua nowae. su lao we ta lothea philo thia sha mea sho haolu. su thia mua thaeso therilu. su thia mua lothea ru siora phirena. su thia mua howai nemo kau womu turema. su thia nulae. su mua thia korua wei [whu thia meropi] miona thorai nai. su nia thia lo phimei ta woraka melira nai.
```

## Translation limits

| Gibran wording | Phi treatment | Remaining difference |
|---|---|---|
| Almitra; he and him | one person asks and a different person answers; `shia` supplies the third person | Phi does not admit an unadapted name into its syntax and does not mark the source's gendered pronouns. |
| beckons; follow; yield | love invites, the listener walks its path, and then accepts love | The invitation and acceptance are less commanding than beckoning followed by obedience or submission. |
| sword hidden among pinions | a long sharp metal thing hidden among wings | The mechanism and danger remain, but only the source gives the thing its weapon name. |
| crowns; crucifies | a head-worn sign of honour; two pieces of wood on which a person is held until death | Material description preserves both images without granting either a general Phi root. |
| pruning | cutting the branches | The purpose and horticultural judgment are supplied by the source rather than by a specialised pruning verb. |
| threshes; sifts; husks; kneads | forceful contact, removal of coverings, movement through a net, and repeated folding and pressure | `salenu` names the resulting unclothed state directly; the four specialised operations remain visible descriptions. |
| God; sacred feast | a sacred spirit and that spirit's sacred festival meal | Phi can describe the religious referent but cannot preserve the source identity inside the sentence. |
| fragment of Life's heart | a small portion of Life's heart | The part-whole relation is direct; Phi does not add the English suggestion of a jagged broken piece. |
| But if | the condition opens with `lu`, and its consequence begins with `thelao` | Slot 0 `lu` must open its sentence, so the preceding contrast is carried by the passage rather than an added coordinator. |
| worthy | deserving love's guidance | The judgment becomes explicit about the outcome that love finds deserved; it does not make human worth conditional. |
| prayer; praise; ecstacy | a blessing, a song of appreciation, and intense joy | The close rendering describes the acts and feeling while the source retains its devotional categories and spelling. |
