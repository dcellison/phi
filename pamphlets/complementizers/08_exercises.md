# Exercises

Practice is where the counting becomes reflex. Work through each exercise type in order; an answer key follows. When your answer differs from the key only in wording, check the boundaries first: the opener, the closer, and how much each one holds.

## Part A: Translation (English to Phi)

Translate each sentence into Phi. Use the complementizers as indicated.

### Declarative (`tha`/`tho`)

1. I know that the sun is beautiful.
2. They believe that rain will come.
3. You feel that the story is true.
4. The friend said that I helped.
5. We understand that they left.
6. She thinks that you are well.
7. I do not know that the friend arrived.
8. They said that the book is good.

### Interrogative (`pha`/`pho`)

9. I wonder whether they are well.
10. She asked whether you saw.
11. We do not know whether rain will come.
12. They wonder whether the friend will return.
13. Do you know whether the story is true?
14. They asked whether I had helped.
15. I don't care whether the story is beautiful.

### Quotative (`sha`/`sho`)

16. They said: "I am leaving."
17. The friend said: "The sun is beautiful."
18. She asked: "Are you well?"
19. I said: "I will return."
20. They announced: "We are here."

## Part C: Error Correction

31. **mia tha shia to wepu tho shelomu**
    (Missing `tho`)

32. **mia pha shia to wepu pho phaelo**
    (Changed second `pha` to `pho`)

33. **shia sha mia wepu sho haolu**
    (Missing `sho`)

34. **whu nophi kealo miona**
    (Removed the borrowed `tho`: `whu` has no closer)

35. **mia tha shia to wepu tho shelomu**
    (Reordered: embedded clause before main verb)

36. **mia tha shia towe nai tho sano**
    (Added `nai`: copula required)

37. **mia pha shia to wepu pho phaelo**
    (Removed `wa`: redundant inside `pha`/`pho`)

38. **thia tha shia to wepu tho sano nela mia pha mia so turema pho phaelo**
    (Simplified: nesting was malformed)

39. **shia sha mia wepu sho haolu**
    (Reversed `sha` and `sho` order)

40. **mia tha shia to wepu tho nohero**
    (Removed extra `tho`: only one closer per opener)

## Part F: Identify the Type

57. Type: **Declarative (`tha`/`tho`)** 
    Embeds: **a statement (that the sun is beautiful)**

58. Type: **Interrogative (`pha`/`pho`)**
    Embeds: **a question (whether the friend will return)**

59. Type: **Quotative (`sha`/`sho`)**
    Embeds: **exact words ("I am well")**

60. Type: **Relative (`whu`)**
    Embeds: **a description of the book (that I saw)**
