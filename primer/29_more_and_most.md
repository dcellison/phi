# 29 · mo

*Warm enough for what? Bigger than what? A degree can be measured on its own, and it can also be held up against something else.* **mo** *takes the place the four degree words take and makes that comparison, with* **sheo** *naming what it is against:*

## I

> **lohau sheo misheko mo whalo nai.**
> **misheko sheo lohau mo maeli nai.**
> **wheo sheo lopia mo serao nai.**

| new word | say it | it means |
|---|---|---|
| mo | mo | (before a quality: more) |
| sheo | she · o | than |
| serao | se · ra · o | old |

*The dog is bigger than the cat. The cat is quieter than the dog. The elder is older than the child. Read the shape once and it stays: the thing, then* sheo *and its rival, then* mo *and the quality.*

## II

> **ha napa mo welao nai.**
> **ha philo mo sulae nai.**
> **luphore mo reshi nai.**

| new word | say it | it means |
|---|---|---|
| reshi | re · shi | fast |

*This bread is better. Today is warmer. The river is faster. When everyone already knows what the comparison is against, Phi leaves it out, exactly as you would.*

## III

> **ha napa mo ko welao nai.**
> **ha thepalu mo ko mioru nai.**
> **wheo nophi mo ko laeno nai.**

| new word | say it | it means |
|---|---|---|
| mioru | mi · o · ru | beautiful |

*Add* **ko** *and the comparison runs out of rivals: the best bread, the most beautiful garden, the longest of the elder's stories. The pair is fixed and always in that order.*

## IV

> **misheko mo ko whalo nolika ma nai.**
> **whekai misheko tha shia mo ko whalo nolika nai tho remo.**

*The cat is not the largest animal. The cat, however, believes that it is. Comparison states a relation and nothing more; it hands out no rank, and any rank the cat has awarded itself was not in the grammar.*

---

*Comparison is a relation, not a verdict. Bigger, faster and older are facts about two things standing side by side, and Phi says them without deciding which one matters.*

*The machinery, when you want it: comparative* mo *and the fixed superlative* mo ko *are the manual's Part IV, chapter 9.*
